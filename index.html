Projects

Things I built to answer a question I had. Source code is on GitHub where the work is mine to share.

Segmented campus network design

Final-year university project, 2025

My undergraduate project redesigned a flat campus network — where a compromised student laptop could reach the finance servers — into segmented VLANs for students, staff, administration and IoT devices. I modelled the topology in Cisco Packet Tracer, wrote the access control lists enforcing traffic between segments, and tested the policy by attempting lateral movement from the student VLAN.

Outcome: a full design document, tested configurations and a written defence of the trade-off between segmentation and administrative overhead. Graded A.

  • Cisco Packet Tracer
  • VLANs
  • ACLs
  • DHCP
  • OSPF

Vulnerability assessment of a deliberately broken shop

Training project, 2026

A full assessment of an OWASP Juice Shop instance, run the way a real engagement would be: scoped, enumerated, exploited, then reported. I chained an injection flaw into an administrative account takeover and documented each step so it could be reproduced by someone who had never seen the application.

Outcome: a fourteen-page report with findings rated by CVSS, evidence screenshots and remediation advice written for developers rather than for auditors.

  • Burp Suite
  • OWASP ZAP
  • Nmap
